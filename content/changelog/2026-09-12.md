[[!meta title="PS/2 keyboard timeout fix"]]
[[!meta date="2026-09-12"]]
[[!pagetemplate template=changelogpage.tmpl]]

- The PS/2 keyboard controller now runs at 4.64 MHz instead of 9.27 MHz, doubling the hardware receive timeout from 2 ms to 4 ms. The 2 ms timeout is too narrow for some boards, causing the controller to drop bytes, resulting in stuck or dropped keys ([issue #3](https://github.com/wmt-os/linux-wmt/issues/3)). Even at the slower rate, the controller has plenty of headroom to sample the PS/2 signals.
- Kernel updated to 6.12.109.
- Refreshed standard and desktop images.
