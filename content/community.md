# Community

[Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/wmt-os/discussions) is the place for help, device reports, and everything else that isn't a concrete bug. Reproducible bugs go to the issue tracker of the repository they concern, listed under [Development](development.html).

## Contact

Security concerns and direct inquiries go to [root@wmt-os.org](mailto:root@wmt-os.org).

This community is small and just getting started. WMT OS is currently a solo project, so please be patient with response times, but don't let that discourage you from participating.
